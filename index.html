Lê Tuân (Tuan Le)

AI/ML Engineer · Computer Vision

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Heudiasyc Laboratory, University of Technology of Compiègne (UTC), France, working with Julien Moreau and Franck Davoine. I work on LiDAR–camera 3D perception for autonomous vehicles: open-vocabulary 3D object detection and, since July 2026, event-based vision with binary neural networks. I have deployed perception models in real time on the lab vehicle (ROS 2) and on an FPGA edge board (INT8, event camera).

I received my PhD from UTC (Roberval Laboratory) in December 2024, advised by Vu Hai Canh and Zohra Cherfi-Boulanger, with the thesis "Semi-supervised Deep Learning for Robust Anomaly Detection in Smart Manufacturing Systems". My PhD covered anomaly detection on industrial sensor signals, control-chart monitoring, product-quality forecasting and explainable defect detection.

I am looking for AI/ML engineer positions in computer vision and robotics for industrial applications, such as predictive maintenance, visual inspection and autonomous systems.

Research

Automatic 3D Annotation of LiDAR Data from Text Prompts
Ongoing research, 2026
video demo

Producing 3D bounding boxes in LiDAR point clouds without human 3D labels, by combining 2D vision foundation models with LiDAR geometry. New categories are specified by text prompts; the demo shows street lamps, traffic signs and trash bins, which are absent from the dataset's label set.

Remaining-Useful-Life Prediction for Turbofan Engines: a Reproducible Benchmark on NASA C-MAPSS
Personal project, 2026
code

About 2,900 training runs comparing literature baselines (DCNN, a re-implemented STA-HPINN) with test-time-memory sequence models (Titans, Memora, Mamba2, DeltaNet) in one shared pipeline, scored with engine-level bootstrap confidence intervals. Operating-condition normalisation mattered 3.4× more than architecture, and two published results could not be reproduced.

Unsupervised Detecting Anomalies in Multivariate Time Series by Robust Convolutional LSTM Encoder–Decoder (RCLED)
Tuan Le, Hai Canh Vu, Amélie Ponchet-Durupt, Nassim Boudaoud, Zohra Cherfi-Boulanger, Thao Nguyen-Trang
Neurocomputing, 2024
paper / code

An autoencoder with convolution, LSTM and self-attention layers, trained with a scheme derived from robust PCA that separates noise from normal patterns, so no anomaly labels are needed. It reaches an F1 score of 0.85 on vibration and temperature data from an industrial machine.

Forecasting Product Quality Using Peephole Long Short-Term Memory
Tuan Le, Hai Canh Vu, Amélie Ponchet-Durupt, Nassim Boudaoud, Zohra Cherfi-Boulanger
CIGI QUALITA MOSIM, 2023  Oral
paper / doi / slides

Forecasting non-linear, non-stationary product-quality time series in manufacturing with peephole LSTMs, as an early signal of the production system's health.

A Deep Learning Approach for Control Chart Patterns (CCPs) Prediction
Tuan Le, Hai Canh Vu, Nassim Boudaoud, Zohra Cherfi-Boulanger, Amélie Ponchet-Durupt, Ho Si Hung Nguyen
European Safety and Reliability Conference (ESREL), 2022  Oral
paper / slides

A CNN that predicts control-chart patterns in simulated SPC time series, covering six process states (normal, cyclic, upward and downward trends and shifts), so that abnormal states can be anticipated before defects occur. Thumbnail: illustration of the six patterns.

New Planning for the 500 kV Vietnamese Grid with High Penetration of Renewable Energy Sources
M. Q. Duong, H. H. Nguyen, Tuan Le, Marco Mussetta
IEEE Milan PowerTech, 2019
paper / PyPSA notebooks

A techno-economic model of Vietnam's 500 kV transmission grid (80 buses) built with PyPSA, for the 2030 plan with large wind and solar capacity. Optimising the power flow before expanding the grid cut the cost of upgrading overloaded lines from $502 million to $95 million.
